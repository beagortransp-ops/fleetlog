Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

This policy explains what personal information FleetLog collects, how we use it and who we share it with. It is written to meet the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA).

1. Information we collect

  • Account info: your email (or phone) used to sign in.
  • Your fleet records: trucks, VINs, mileage, service and repair records, parts, inspections, driver names you enter, signatures, shop names and costs.
  • Billing info: your plan, billing cycle, payment status and the time you agreed to the renewal terms. Card details are entered on Stripe's page; we do not receive your full card number.
  • Messages: your name, email and message when you contact us.
  • Technical info: IP address and basic server logs, used for security (for example, limiting wrong sign-in attempts).

2. How we use it

To run FleetLog and show your records, to process payments and manage your plan, to answer support and cancel requests, to send receipts and important account notices, and to keep the service secure. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

Only with service providers that help us run FleetLog, and only as needed:

  • Stripe – payments and subscriptions.
  • Resend – sends emails, such as your support and cancel messages.
  • Render – hosts the website and stores the data.
  • Supabase – sign-in codes, where sign-in is turned on.

We may also share information if required by law, or as part of a sale of the business (with notice to you).

4. Cookies and browser storage

FleetLog stores small items in your browser (like your sign-in session) so the app works. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

5. Do Not Track

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" signal. FleetLog does not track you across other websites and does not allow third parties to do so, so we treat all visitors the same whether or not this signal is on.

6. Reviewing and changing your information

You can view, edit, export (CSV) and delete your fleet records in the app at any time. To correct or delete your account information or ask what we have, email . We will answer within 30 days.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your records while your account is open. After you ask us to delete your account, we delete your data, except what we must keep for tax or legal reasons (like payment records).

8. Security

We use HTTPS and access codes to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your sign-in code private.

9. Children

FleetLog is for businesses and adults. It is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date. For important changes we will also email you or show a notice in the app.
