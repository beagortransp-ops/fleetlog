Cancel anytime, online

You can cancel your subscription at any time in FleetLog: Settings → Plans & billing → Manage or cancel subscription. Or email .

What happens when you cancel

Your plan stops renewing. You keep full access until the end of the month or year you already paid for. Your records stay saved and you can export them.

Refunds

Payments are not refunded for partly used months or years, except where required by law. If you were charged by mistake (for example, a double charge), email us and we will fix it.

Free trials

The Pro yearly plan has a 10-day free trial. If you cancel before the trial ends, you are not charged. If you do not cancel, $299 is charged when the trial ends.
